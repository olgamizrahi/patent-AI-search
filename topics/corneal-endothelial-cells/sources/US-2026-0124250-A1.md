**Scope fit:** Independent claims are directed to molecular/marker-based methods for assessing cultured corneal endothelial cell (CEC) quality — measuring expression of genetic/protein markers (VMO1, THBS2 to identify lower-grade/non-therapy-grade cells; CGNL1, CD166, CD56, CD44, CD10 for a comprehensive quality profile), including immunofluorescence/FACS detection, marker-based selection/sorting to enrich native-like CECs, and machine-learning-based automated classification, to produce and identify "therapy-grade" cultured CEC populations for transplantation. Fits the new biomarker/RNA-seq/molecular-characterization inclusion criterion added to scope 2026-09-08.
