**Scope fit:** Independent claims are directed to methods for enriching and isolating human corneal endothelial cells (HCECs) using surface-marker-based affinity selection: positive selection with reagents that selectively bind HCECs while distinguishing them from contaminant cells (e.g. keratocytes, lower-utility endothelial cells), negative selection targeting unwanted populations, and combined approaches, using affinity reagents/kits (antibodies or aptamers coupled to labels or solid matrices), based on surface markers including CD56, CD248, and CAR expression patterns, to produce enriched compositions of functionally superior HCECs for therapeutic/research use.
