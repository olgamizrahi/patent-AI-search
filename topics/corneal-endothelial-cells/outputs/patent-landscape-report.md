## 1. Scope summary

This report covers patents and published patent applications relating to corneal endothelial cell (CEC) technology, searched across US, EP, and WO sources with filing/publication dates from approximately 2016-01-01 to 2026-09-08, per the approved [`patent-scope.md`](../patent-scope.md). Three technology clusters were searched: (1) cell therapy and injectable-formulation products for treating corneal endothelial dysfunction (e.g., Fuchs' endothelial dystrophy, bullous keratopathy); (2) in vitro culture, expansion, and differentiation methods and media for corneal endothelial cells derived from donor tissue or iPSCs; and (3) RNA-seq, gene-expression, surface-marker, and other molecular methods for identifying, classifying, sorting, or quality-grading corneal endothelial cells. Legal status for every record was checked against Google Patents on 2026-09-08. Sixteen patents/applications survived both source verification and claims-text screening and are analyzed below; one additional verified source record (EP4431596A4) was excluded from the claims table because its claim text could not be retrieved from any accessible source (see Section 5). A further set of candidate records was identified and excluded during searching for reasons documented in [`sources/README.md`](../sources/README.md) and summarized in Section 5.

## 2. Landscape overview

The 16 analyzed patents/applications fall into three technology clusters as defined in scope. Several patents span more than one cluster; these are noted under each cluster they touch.

### 2.1 Cell therapy / injectable formulation cluster

This cluster covers claims to cell suspensions, formulations, and treatment methods for administering corneal endothelial cells (or agents that act on them) to treat corneal endothelial disease.

- **Keio University** holds [US10501725B2](../outputs/claims-table.md#1-us10501725b2) (Active), claiming a pharmaceutical composition of a corneal endothelial cell (or cell-sphere) suspension combined with a viscoelastic substance (hyaluronic acid and/or chondroitin sulfate) in defined concentration and mixing ratios, for transplantation into the anterior chamber, and a corresponding engraftment-improvement method claim.
- **Senju Pharmaceutical / The Doshisha / Kyoto Prefectural Public University Corporation (Kyoto PUC)** hold a family pair: [US11633477B2](../outputs/claims-table.md#5-us11633477b2) (Active) and its EP counterpart [EP3213762B1](../outputs/claims-table.md#6-ep3213762b1) (Active), both claiming administration of laminins (or fragments, containing an α5 and/or γ1 chain) as a therapeutic/prophylactic agent for corneal endothelial disease, disorder, or condition — the US claim recites co-administration with a corneal endothelial cell suspension, while the EP claim is drafted in EPC "agent/medical use" format.
- **The Doshisha** holds [US11445723B2](../outputs/claims-table.md#8-us11445723b2) (Active), claiming a method and container for preserving corneal endothelial cells/corneal-endothelium-like cells in a cell-suspension state for 24–72 hours such that the cells can be administered "without additional processing or culturing" — i.e., a preservation step feeding directly into cell injection therapy.
- **The Doshisha / Actualeyes Inc.** hold [US20250000083A1](../outputs/claims-table.md#10-us20250000083a1) (Pending), a large multi-claim family covering slow-rate cryopreservation methods, frozen-formulation compositions (e.g., a formulation with less than 7% DMSO, and a separate formulation combining a ROCK inhibitor with frozen cells), and an end-to-end cell-injection-therapy method claim (freeze → maintain → thaw → administer).
- **Senju Pharmaceutical** holds [US20230210864A1](../outputs/claims-table.md#2-us20230210864a1) (Pending), whose sole independent claim, as issued, is a method of treating corneal endothelial dysfunction caused by elevated intraocular pressure by administering a Rho-kinase inhibitor — a small-molecule treatment-method claim rather than a cultured-cell composition claim (see judgment-call note in Section 7).
- **Kyoto PUC** holds [US20190083543A1](../outputs/claims-table.md#12-us20190083543a1) (Pending), which spans all three clusters: independent claims to a human functional corneal endothelial cell and a medicament comprising it (both defined by capacity to elicit corneal endothelial function when infused into the anterior chamber), a manufacturing-method claim, and quality-control/marker-method claims (see below).

Legal-status mix in this cluster: three Active/in-force patents (US10501725B2, US11633477B2, EP3213762B1), one Active patent tied to a preservation step (US11445723B2), and three Pending applications (US20230210864A1, US20250000083A1, US20190083543A1). The Senju/Doshisha/Kyoto PUC and Doshisha/Actualeyes groupings appear to represent a related institutional cluster (overlapping inventors/assignees across several culture, preservation, and therapy-method families in this report).

### 2.2 Culture/expansion method cluster

This cluster covers claims to media, protocols, and process steps for culturing, expanding, or differentiating corneal endothelial cells from donor tissue or iPSCs.

- **Keio University** holds two additional patents beyond its formulation patent above: [US9347042B2](../outputs/claims-table.md#7-us9347042b2) (Active), claiming differentiation of iPSC-derived neural crest stem cells into corneal endothelial cells using defined media (GSK3 inhibitor, retinoic acid, TGFb2, insulin, and/or ROCK inhibitor in staged serum-containing or serum-free protocols), and [US12350294B2](../outputs/claims-table.md#13-us12350294b2) (Active), claiming a specific defined medium (serum replacement, IGF1, a STAT3 activator, and adrenal-gland hormone, explicitly excluding bFGF or a ROCK inhibitor) and corresponding method for producing corneal endothelial substitute cells from iPS cells, characterized by expression of ATP1A1, ZO-1, N-cadherin, and PITX2.
- **Singapore Health Services Pte Ltd** holds [US10017735B2](../outputs/claims-table.md#3-us10017735b2) (Active), claiming a three-stage culture method (stabilize in Human Endothelial-SFM maintenance medium → propagate/expand in a proliferative medium → re-stabilize in the maintenance medium).
- **JCR Pharmaceuticals / The Doshisha / Kyoto PUC** hold [US10174284B2](../outputs/claims-table.md#4-us10174284b2) (Active), claiming culture of human corneal endothelial cells using a medium derived from mesenchymal-stem-cell-conditioned medium containing chondroitin sulfate at a defined concentration range, including a variant using a filtration-concentrated version of that conditioned medium.
- **The Doshisha** ([US11445723B2](../outputs/claims-table.md#8-us11445723b2), above) and **Kyoto PUC** ([US12550891B2](../outputs/claims-table.md#9-us12550891b2), Active) both claim process steps that combine culture/handling with downstream use: US12550891B2 claims culturing at a defined seeding density (300–500 cells/mm²) in a ROCK-inhibitor-containing medium, then harvesting at a specific window defined by CD44 expression level relative to its post-seeding maximum, followed by suspension and preservation — combining a culture-method claim with a CD44 biomarker-timed harvest step.
- **Kyoto PUC**'s [US20190083543A1](../outputs/claims-table.md#12-us20190083543a1) (Pending, discussed above) also includes an independent manufacturing-method claim (claim 19) directed to growing, maturating, and differentiating a corneal-endothelial-tissue-derived cell or progenitor cell, directly or via a dedifferentiation step.

Legal-status mix in this cluster: four Active/in-force patents (US9347042B2, US12350294B2, US10017735B2, US10174284B2), plus the Active US12550891B2 and US11445723B2 already counted above, and the Pending US20190083543A1. The Keio University patents form one institutional cluster; the Kyoto PUC/Doshisha/JCR/Senju group forms a second, larger institutional cluster spanning culture media, preservation, and therapy claims.

### 2.3 Biomarker / molecular-characterization method cluster

This cluster covers claims to marker-based (surface-marker, gene-expression, or other molecular) methods for identifying, classifying, sorting, or quality-grading corneal endothelial cells.

- **Kyoto PUC / Actualeyes Inc. / CorneaGen LLC / Aurion Biotech Inc.** hold [US10908161B2](../outputs/claims-table.md#11-us10908161b2) (Active), whose sole independent claim is a method for differentiating non-fibroblastic from fibroblastic-phenotype corneal endothelial cells in a sample by detecting defined panels of cell-surface markers (e.g., CD166, HLA-A2, and others associated with the non-fibroblastic phenotype; CD26, CD56, CD44, CD73, and others associated with the fibroblastic phenotype), with no transplantation or formulation step recited in the claim itself.
- **Universiteit Maastricht / Academisch Ziekenhuis Maastricht** hold a family pair: [US20260124250A1](../outputs/claims-table.md#14-us20260124250a1) (Pending) and its EP counterpart [EP4655392A2](../outputs/claims-table.md#15-ep4655392a2) (Pending), both claiming a method for determining cultured-CEC quality by measuring THBS2 and/or VMO1 gene expression (a THBS2+/VMO1+ profile indicating non-therapy-grade quality), plus a second independent claim to a computer-implemented classification method comparing measured expression data (optionally including CGNL1, CD166, CD56, CD44, CD10) against reference therapy-grade/non-therapy-grade expression profiles.
- **Emmetrope Ophthalmics LLC** holds [US10655102B2](../outputs/claims-table.md#16-us10655102b2) (Active), claiming a method of enriching human corneal endothelial cells using sequential positive-affinity-reagent selection (antibodies to CD56, CD166, CAR, and/or CD248 surface proteins) with an optional negative-selection step against fibroblastic-transformed cells, plus corresponding kit and composition claims built on the same marker panel.
- **Kyoto PUC**'s [US20190083543A1](../outputs/claims-table.md#12-us20190083543a1) (Pending, discussed above) also includes independent quality-control/process-control claims (claims 26 and 29) reciting panels of cell-function indicators — surface markers, secreted proteins, SASP-related proteins, miRNA, exosomes, metabolites, cell size/density, and defined FACS/ELISA/functional-assay thresholds — for assessing cultured CEC quality.
- **Kyoto PUC**'s [US12550891B2](../outputs/claims-table.md#9-us12550891b2) (Active, discussed above) also incorporates a CD44-expression-timed harvest step within its culture-method claim.

Legal-status mix in this cluster: two Active/in-force patents (US10908161B2, US10655102B2, plus US12550891B2 counted above), two Pending applications from a single family (US20260124250A1, EP4655392A2), and the Pending US20190083543A1. The Maastricht family represents a newer entrant (added to scope 2026-09-08) distinct from the Japan-based institutional cluster that dominates the other two clusters.

## 5. Legal-status note

Legal status for all records in this report reflects a Google Patents check performed on **2026-09-08**. Patent legal status (particularly for pending applications, and for maintenance-fee-dependent "Active" status on granted patents) can change after that date. In particular:

- **Pending applications** (US20230210864A1, US20250000083A1, US20190083543A1, US20260124250A1, EP4655392A2) may issue with claim scope different from — narrower, broader, or differently worded than — what is currently published and reproduced in this report and its accompanying claims table. Prosecution amendments are common and this report does not attempt to predict them.
- **"Active" status** on granted patents reflects the checked date only and depends on continued maintenance-fee payment; it is not a guarantee of continued force through the patent's full term.
- This report does not track post-grant proceedings (e.g., reexamination, opposition, IPR) that may be pending or later filed against any listed patent.

## 7. Judgment calls made in drafting this report

- For **US20230210864A1**, the claims table already flagged that independent claim 1, as issued, is a Rho-kinase-inhibitor treatment-method claim rather than a cultured-cell-composition or culture-method claim. This report's Section 2.1 places the patent primarily in the cell-therapy/treatment-method cluster and describes its claim scope narrowly as "administering a Rho-kinase inhibitor to treat corneal endothelial dysfunction caused by elevated intraocular pressure" — matching the verbatim claim language rather than the broader title/family framing ("promoting corneal endothelial cell adhesion"), to avoid overstating claim scope toward a culture-method characterization the claim text does not support.
- For **US10908161B2**, the claims table resolved a screening flag between the biomarker-inclusion criterion and the diagnostic-only exclusion criterion. This report describes the claim purely as a marker-detection/differentiation method (consistent with the claim's actual language) and does not characterize it as a "diagnostic" or attribute to it any therapeutic-selection function beyond what the claim recites, even though the specification and the patent's title suggest a downstream therapeutic-quality-control use.
- For **US11445723B2** and **US12550891B2**, both of which combine a culture/preservation process step with a biomarker- or therapy-adjacent element, this report lists them under multiple clusters rather than forcing a single-cluster characterization, consistent with how the claims table already flagged their cross-cutting scope.
- No new patents, technology areas, or claim characterizations beyond those already documented in `patent-scope.md`, `sources/README.md`, and `claims-table.md` were introduced.
